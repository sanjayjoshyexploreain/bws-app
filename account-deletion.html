Account & Data Deletion

How to request account deletion

At Build & Weld Services (BWS), we respect your privacy and provide a simple way for you to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

To delete your account, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the BWS Portal mobile application and log in to your account.
  2. Scroll down to the Settings section on your Dashboard.
  3. Click on the Account Deletion Request button.
  4. Confirm the prompt to submit your request.

Once the button is clicked, your account deletion request is sent directly to the BWS Human Resources team. HR will process your request, verify the status, and permanently delete your account and related data from our database in accordance with our retention policies.

If you no longer have access to the app, you may also contact your BWS Manager or the IT Department directly to initiate the deletion process.
